🌎 Our Planet

We live on planet Earth, the third planet from the Sun.

☀️

The Sun is a star. It gives Earth light and heat.

🗺️

Earth has 7 continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia.

🌊

The Pacific Ocean is the largest ocean on Earth.

🌤️

On a clear day the sky looks blue.

🐄 Animals & Nature

Animals and plants give us many useful things.

🥛

A cow gives us milk.

🍯

Bees make honey from the nectar of flowers.

🌱

Plants need sunlight, water and air to grow.

🐘

The elephant is the largest land animal.

📅 Everyday Facts

Some numbers we use every day.

7️⃣

A week has 7 days.

🗓️

A year has 12 months.

💧

Water boils at 100 °C (at sea level).

🌈

A rainbow has 7 colours.
